About Us

Our Story

Founded in 2011, Bright Future Academy started with just 45 students in a rented building in Nairobi. Today, we are a thriving institution with over 500 learners, a dedicated faculty of 35+ teachers, and a reputation for academic excellence and character development.

Mission

To provide quality, affordable education that empowers learners to become innovative, ethical, and globally competitive citizens.

Vision

To be a leading centre of holistic education in East Africa, renowned for academic excellence and character formation.

Academics

Our Programs

A seamless learning journey from early years through junior secondary.

Early Years

Play-based learning for ages 3–5. We focus on social skills, literacy, numeracy, and creative expression.

Subjects: Language, Numbers, Environmental, Creative Arts, Music & Movement.

Primary

Grades 1–6 following the Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) with a strong foundation in literacy and numeracy.

Subjects: English, Kiswahili, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, CRE, Creative Arts, P.E.

Junior Secondary

Grades 7–9. Learners explore diverse pathways with a focus on critical thinking and specialisation.

Subjects: English, Kiswahili, Mathematics, Integrated Science, Social Studies, Pre-Technical, Business, Agriculture, CRE, Sports.
